# Visual Identity

## Style Prompt

Microsoft Fluent-inspired enterprise product launch film for Douyin Live Institution Edition. Supplied UI recordings and screenshots are the visual truth. Preserve real navigation, tabs, tables, cards, hierarchy, typography scale and product colors. Use a pale blue-white canvas, restrained acrylic framing, soft layered shadows, localized blue emphasis and clean bottom caption bands. Keep a fixed centered composition; motion is limited to UI emphasis, cursor feedback, controlled zoom inside the source screen and smooth Fluent transitions.

## Colors

- Canvas: `#EEF4FF`
- Surface: `#F8FBFF`
- Acrylic: `rgba(244,248,255,.78)`
- Primary blue: `#3378F6`
- Deep blue: `#123A72`
- Secondary text: `#5E718B`
- Warm alert: `#FF6B5F`

If the real product UI has its own primary brand color, preserve it inside the UI. Do not recolor screenshots or recordings to match this palette.

## Typography

- Primary Chinese/UI: `PingFang SC`
- Fallback: `Microsoft YaHei, sans-serif`
- Preserve source UI typography inside screenshots and recordings.
- Use the supplied official “直播服务平台・机构版” logo without redrawing it.

## Layout

- Default canvas proposal: `1920×1080`, `30fps`.
- UI is the hero and normally occupies 88%–92% of the canvas width.
- Reserve the bottom safe area for captions and the bottom-right watermark.
- Use acrylic framing and soft shadows only outside the real UI.

## Motion

- Fixed center composition; no decorative camera travel or perspective fly-throughs.
- `power3.out` for entrances and `power3.inOut` for transitions.
- Use focus rings, short click feedback, subtle scale emphasis and acrylic masks.
- Keep AI summaries, data tables and operation results on screen long enough to read.

## Audio

- Use the bundled product-promo music by default.
- Voice remains clear and forward; music stays perceptible underneath.
- Add restrained UI sounds to meaningful visible actions and the bundled sting to the end card.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not redesign the product as an abstract dashboard.
- Do not cover important UI text with captions or decorative panels.
- Do not use dark cyberpunk colors, particles, neon grids or unrelated AI imagery.
- Do not cut away before an operation or its narration finishes.
- Do not use large floating marketing cards that compete with the real UI.
